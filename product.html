Kikuyu Lawn

Kikuyu grass is perfect for covering a big area of land. We start by planting it on the land and letting it grow. Once it's grown, we cut it into pieces that are one meter long and half a meter wide. This helps us manage it neatly. After that, we roll up the cut grass and get it ready to be delivered to you. You have two options for getting Kikuyu grass:

  1. Delivery which costs R30 for every square meter.
  2. Delivery and planting, which costs R35.

kwik Lawn

If you're looking for a quick solution, Kwik grass is similar to Kikuyu grass but faster. We plant it on the land, just like with Kikuyu grass, and let it grow. After it's grown, we cut it into pieces that are one meter long and half a meter wide. This makes it easy to handle. Then, we roll it up neatly and get it ready for delivery. You also have two choices for Kwik grass:

  1. Delivery only, which costs R30 per square meter.
  2. Delivery and planting, which is R35.

Please keep in mind that after the grass is delivered or planted, we are not responsible for its condition or any events that may happen to it. Proper care and maintenance will be your responsibility once it's in your possession. Be sure to water it properly to ensure its health and growth.

Both Kikuyu and Kwik grass are great options, and we're here to make it simple for you to get the grass you need for your project.
